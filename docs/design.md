# Design contract

Keep the light Melt system: almost-white paper (#fafafa), white surfaces, graphite text, quiet periwinkle controls and self-hosted Manrope. Use clear verbs and concrete financial labels. No decorative blobs or promises of global visual uniqueness.

The primary sequence is one task, its allowance, an isolated browser and its returned result. The session detail puts the permission and amounts beside the browser. Activity and advanced recovery stay secondary. Preserve exact financial strings; animation must not round balances or imply a transaction succeeded.

Custom interactions: SessionSeal opens a paper sleeve for an active task and settles a receipt when closed. Its activity bars move only during actual execution and pause offscreen or in hidden documents. CapacityRibbon encodes the remaining fraction, including fractional ETH amounts, and gently lifts nearby folds under a pointer. Pointer movement is optional; native touch and keyboard interaction remain intact. Reduced motion shows final state without repeated motion.

Rare UI's Gooey Nav supports movement between Sessions, Receipts and Developers. It shares application colours and spacing. The retained Animated Counter is available but unused in current financial displays. See ui-credits.md for source and license details.

Visual QA covers settled desktop and 390px mobile states, control visibility, overflow, and the live financial lifecycle. Preserve calm spacing and useful information; avoid extra slogans, fictitious statistics or motion unrelated to the task.
